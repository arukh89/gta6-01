Who runs Solmaris tonight?

Three crew, one city. Money, jobs, Respect and progress are shared — each keeps their own skills, weapons, health and spot in the city. The others wait at the safehouse: press T there to switch.

How to play

Follow the gold marker on the minimap to work through nine story jobs for Dario, from a first errand to the Heatwave finale. Between jobs, take taxi fares at the blue markers, race from the purple marker, grab cash drops and Tide Tapes, and explore Solmaris on foot or in any car you can reach. The gun shop sells an SMG and armour; the garage repairs and repaints your car once Dario trusts you with it. Three characters share the crew — Lena Cruz, Ivy Solano and Marco Vane: pick one when you start a new game, and press T at the safehouse to switch. Money, jobs, Respect and progress are shared; each keeps their own skills, weapons, health and spot in the city.

Crime raises your wanted stars — but only when someone sees it. Shoot or steal with no witness around and the police never hear about it; a car alarm can give you away. Once the stars are up, break line of sight and stay hidden: the stars blink while the police search, and each star cools off after 30–90 seconds depending on the level. A new car while hidden cools one star. The Spray Barn only works when the police have lost sight of you and you drive in ($150). Getting busted costs all your ammo and armour; running out of health sends you to the clinic, which takes 5% of your cash. Health only regrows to 50 on its own — snacks, the clinic, and the safehouse take you past that. Police cars chase you, officers come on foot, a helicopter joins at three stars (you can shoot it down), and at four stars SWAT and roadblocks join in. At five stars, one more witnessed crime brings HEAT: a sixth red star, more cruisers, faster SWAT and roadblocks, and stars that cool half as fast. Hold X for slow-mo — the world crawls while you stay fast, until the meter runs dry. You can swim: walk into the bay at 60% speed, watch your stamina, and get out before it runs dry. The two skiffs at the marina and the lighthouse are free to board with E — they only sail on water, and the police cannot follow you out there.
