Golden hour in Solmaris. Lena Cruz owes Dario one last favour, the whole city is awake, and every car on the street is a way out. Take the jobs, keep the heat off, and make the bay yours.

WASD / arrows — walk and drive · Mouse — aim · Click — shoot / punch · E — steal or enter a car · Shift — sprint / handbrake · 1 fists, 2 pistol · P pause · M sound · Progress saves automatically.

Every name, character, street and sound in this game is original. No assets from any commercial game.

How to play

Follow the gold marker on the minimap to work through six story jobs for Dario, from a first errand to the Heatwave finale. Between jobs, take taxi fares at the blue markers, grab cash drops, and explore Solmaris on foot or in any car you can reach.

Crime raises your wanted stars. Police cars chase you and officers come on foot. Break line of sight and stay hidden to cool down, or pay $150 at the Spray Barn to wipe the slate clean. Getting busted costs you $200 and your heat; running out of health sends you to the clinic and costs $150.
